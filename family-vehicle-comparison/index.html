    How to read this

    • Annual energy cost is an estimate. Gas and hybrid: miles ÷ EPA combined MPG × gas price. EV: miles × EPA kWh per 100 miles ÷ 100 × electricity rate. Real costs move with weather, speed, tires and charging losses.
    • MPG, kWh per 100 miles and range are EPA combined figures from fueleconomy.gov for the matching model year and drivetrain. The EX90 figure is the EPA listing for the Twin Motor on 20 and 22 inch wheels.
    • Price is the listed MSRP for that model year and trim from the source linked on each card, checked Oct 18, 2026. Destination is called out where the source states it. Prices are not transaction or used prices. The 2024 models are prior model years and now sell at different prices.
    • Kia EV9 Wind: Kia's 2024 features chart lists the exterior V2L (vehicle to load) outlet on Land and GT-Line, not Wind.
