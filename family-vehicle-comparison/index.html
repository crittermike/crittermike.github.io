        How to read this

        • Energy cost is an estimate. Gas and hybrid: miles ÷ EPA combined MPG × gas price. EV: miles × EPA kWh/100 mi ÷ 100 × electricity rate. Weather, speed and charging losses move real costs.
        • Cost to own = listed MSRP + destination + energy over the years you set. It leaves out tax, insurance, maintenance, depreciation and discounts. The 2024 models are prior model years and now sell for different prices.
        • Gas and hybrid range = tank size × EPA combined MPG. EV range is the EPA figure.
        • Ranking scores each vehicle 0–1 against the others on each factor, then weights them. Space averages 2nd-row legroom, 3rd-row legroom, cargo behind the 3rd row and max cargo. Features counts standard items in the feature table (options count 0.3). Safety uses the IIHS award for that model year.
        • Federal EV tax credits ended for vehicles acquired after Sept 30, 2025. The 2024 EV9 (Korea-built) and EX90 Plus (over the $80k SUV cap) did not qualify for the purchase credit anyway.
        • Interior photos are often a higher trim or a non-US model. Captions say so.
