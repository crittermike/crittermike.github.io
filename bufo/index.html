npm start

Get a TypeSafe key. Never paste your key into chat or commit it.

Connecting makes one tiny test request, then enables suggestions after a 700 ms pause. Each suggestion scores the entire catalog in batches and can take several seconds. Your key isn't saved to disk or browser storage. Account credits or billing may be required.
