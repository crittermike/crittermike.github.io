Beef enchiladas

Serves
6
Prep
20 min
Cook
25 min
Protein
45g
Calories
620
Kids
Yes

Ingredients

Tap to check off as you go
  • 2 lb ground beef (85/15)
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning (or 1 packet)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 (16 oz) can refried beans
  • 1/2 cup red enchilada sauce (from the batch below)
  • 2 (10 oz) cans red enchilada sauce (mild), or 20 oz homemade
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 12 flour tortillas (8 inch)
  • 3 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
  • Sour cream, for serving
  • Tortilla chips, for serving

Method

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
  2. Brown the beef in a large skillet over medium-high, breaking it up, about 8 minutes. Drain the fat.
  3. Add the onion, cook 4 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, taco seasoning, and cumin, cook 1 minute.
  4. Stir in the refried beans and 1/2 cup of the enchilada sauce. Cook 2 minutes until it holds together. Remove from heat.
  5. Whisk the remaining enchilada sauce with the tomato paste and cumin.
  6. Spread 3/4 cup of sauce across the bottom of the dish.
  7. Warm the tortillas 20 seconds in the microwave under a damp towel so they roll without cracking.
  8. Fill each tortilla with about 1/3 cup of the beef mixture plus a pinch of cheese. Roll tight, place seam-side down in the dish. 12 fit in two rows.
  9. Pour the remaining sauce over the top, spreading to cover the ends. Scatter the remaining cheese over everything.
  10. Bake uncovered 20 to 25 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the sauce bubbles at the edges.
  11. Rest 5 minutes before serving. Top with sour cream.

Notes & pitfalls

Notes

  • Reserve a portion of the seasoned beef before it hits the beans and sauce, and serve it dry alongside tortilla chips.
  • Corn tortillas work but must be fried 10 seconds per side in oil first or they split.
  • Macros are per 2 enchiladas.
  • Assembles a day ahead. Cover, refrigerate, add 10 minutes to the bake.
  • Leftovers reheat well covered at 350°F for 15 minutes. Microwave makes the tortillas gummy.
