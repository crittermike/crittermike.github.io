Chicken enchiladas (canned sauce)

Serves
6
Prep
20 min
Cook
50 min
Kids
Yes

Ingredients

Tap to check off as you go
  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 (16 oz) can plain refried beans; check label for peppers
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 (10 oz) cans mild red enchilada sauce
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 12 flour tortillas (8 inch)
  • 3 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
  • Sour cream, for serving
  • Tortilla chips, for serving

Method

  1. Place the chicken and 1 1/2 cups broth in a covered skillet or saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer. Turn the chicken once if it is not fully submerged. Simmer gently until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Timing depends on thickness, usually about 15–25 minutes. Transfer to a plate, rest 5 minutes, then shred.
  2. Heat the oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  3. Measure 1/2 cup enchilada sauce for the filling. Stir the remaining 1/2 tsp cumin into the rest of the sauce; no separate sauce cooking is needed.
  4. In a large skillet, cook the onion over medium heat with a splash of reserved broth until soft, about 4 minutes. Add the garlic, remaining 1 tsp cumin, and 1/2 tsp oregano; cook 30 seconds.
  5. Stir in the shredded chicken and refried beans with the reserved 1/2 cup enchilada sauce. Warm for about 2 minutes. Before adding beans and sauce, set aside a plain portion of chicken if a separate component is useful.
  6. Spread about 3/4 cup sauce in the baking dish. Warm the tortillas under a damp towel in the microwave for about 20 seconds so they roll without cracking.
  7. Fill each tortilla with about 1/3 cup chicken mixture and a little cheese. Roll seam-side down; arrange 12 in two rows.
  8. Pour the remaining sauce over the tortillas and scatter the remaining cheese on top.
  9. Bake uncovered for 20–25 minutes, until hot throughout, the cheese is melted, and sauce bubbles at the edges. Rest 5 minutes. Serve with sour cream and tortilla chips.

Notes & pitfalls

Notes

  • Macros have not been calculated for this chicken adaptation.
  • The canned enchilada sauce may contain chili peppers or paprika; check the label if avoiding them is important.
  • Corn tortillas can be used, but warm them carefully or they may crack.
  • Assemble up to one day ahead, cover and refrigerate, then add about 10 minutes to the bake time.
  • Reheat leftovers covered at 350°F for about 15 minutes. Microwaving can make the tortillas gummy.
