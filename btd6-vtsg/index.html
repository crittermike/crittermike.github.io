How to Get the Vengeful True Sun God

The VTSG is the strongest non-Paragon tower in the game. It is not an upgrade you buy. It is a hidden transformation that only happens when you set up several things in exactly the right order.

The short version

  1. Build a Sun Temple with maxed sacrifices in 3 of the 4 tower categories.
  2. Place an Anti-Bloon and a Legend of the Night outside its range.
  3. Feed the Sun Temple maxed sacrifices in all 4 categories.
  4. Upgrade it to True Sun God. It eats the other two Super Monkeys and becomes the VTSG.

Before you start: the checklist

If any one of these is wrong, you get a plain True Sun God and you have wasted a lot of money. Check all five.

  • Every Super Monkey upgrade is unlocked (all three paths, tier 5).
  • The Monkey Knowledge There Can Be Only One is unlocked in the Magic tree.
  • Monkey Knowledge is enabled. Look for the green dot next to the Knowledge button on the main menu. Red dot means it is off.
  • You are not playing CHIMPS. CHIMPS disables Monkey Knowledge, so a VTSG is impossible there.
  • If it is a Challenge, the challenge rules are not disabling Monkey Knowledge.
Sandbox You can make a VTSG in Sandbox, but only after you have already made one in a real game at least once.

How sacrificing actually works

When you upgrade a Super Monkey into a Sun Temple, it destroys every tower inside its circle and absorbs their power. Same thing again when the Sun Temple becomes a True Sun God.

The game sorts every tower into one of four categories. What matters is the total cash you spent on towers and upgrades in each category inside the circle.

CategoryExamples

SupportBanana Farm, Spike Factory, Monkey Village, Engineer, Beast Handler
Watch out Ninja Monkey counts as Magic. A lot of people assume it is Military because of how it plays. It is not. Dartling Gunner is the Military one people forget.

The magic number is $50,000

A category is "maxed" once you have spent $50,001 or more on towers of that category inside the circle. Anything above that is wasted, so aim for just over.

Two things that save you money
  • The exact towers do not matter. Only the total cash per category. Use whatever is cheapest to reach $50k.
  • On Easy difficulty towers cost less, so you have to buy more of them to hit $50k. Hard mode towers cost more, so you need fewer.

The full walkthrough

  1. Place the Super Monkey that will become the VTSG Pick a spot with plenty of open ground around it. You need room for a lot of towers inside its circle, twice. Do not put it in a cramped corner.
  2. Fill its circle with $150,000+ of sacrifices Pick any three categories and spend at least $50,001 on each, all inside the Super Monkey's range circle. A common easy trio is Primary, Military, and Magic. Tap the Super Monkey to see its range circle before you place anything.
  3. Upgrade to Sun Temple (4-x-x) That is the fourth upgrade on the top path. It eats everything in the circle. Check the temple's info panel afterward to confirm it registered three maxed categories.
  4. Place two more Super Monkeys OUTSIDE the temple's range Upgrade one to The Anti-Bloon (x-5-x, middle path) and the other to Legend of the Night (x-x-5, bottom path). They must be outside the circle or the temple will eat them as ordinary sacrifices and the whole thing fails.
  5. Fill the circle again with $200,000+ of sacrifices This time you need all four categories maxed, including Support. Same $50,001 minimum each.
  6. Upgrade Sun Temple → True Sun God (5-x-x) It transforms into a normal True Sun God first. Wait a second. It then reaches out, absorbs the Anti-Bloon and the Legend of the Night, and becomes the VTSG. The model and portrait change to a dark, glowing version.

Common mistakes

Why yours turned out normal
  • Anti-Bloon or Legend of the Night was inside the circle. They got sacrificed as regular towers instead of being absorbed.
  • A VTSG already exists on the map. Only one can exist at a time. A second attempt always produces a plain True Sun God.
  • Monkey Knowledge was off, or you were in CHIMPS.
  • A category came up just short of $50,001. Being at $49,900 in one category silently downgrades the result.
  • Support discount trap. If you sacrificed Support towers to the Sun Temple, it now hands out price discounts to towers in its range. Everything you buy for the second round costs less, which means it also counts for less. You have to buy more to compensate.

Quick way to check your totals

If you are unsure whether a category hit the threshold, look at the sell values of the towers in the circle. Sell value is 70% of what you spent, so each category should show at least $35,000 in combined sell value. With the Better Sell Deals knowledge, aim for $37,500.

What you get

  • Main attack does +35 damage over a normal True Sun God.
  • Every bonus attack it inherited from the sacrifices does double damage.
  • Its Sun Avatars become Vengeful Sun Avatars.
  • If Adora is on the map and already transformed, she becomes Vengeful Adora, which unlocks the Strangely Adorable achievement.
  • You cannot buy another Anti-Bloon or Legend of the Night while the VTSG is alive. Selling the VTSG unlocks them again.
Reality check This costs roughly $350,000 across both stages plus the Super Monkeys themselves. It is a late freeplay project, round 100 and beyond. Do not try it in the first 60 rounds.
